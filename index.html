    How to Play

    All 52 cards are dealt. Whoever holds the Ace of Spades ♠ leads the first trick.

    Everyone must follow the suit that was led, if they can.

    If everyone follows suit, the trick is discarded and the highest card leads next.

    If you can't follow suit, throw any other card. That's a THULLA! The player with the highest card of the led suit picks up the whole trick.

    No Thulla can happen on the first trick.

    Empty your hand to be safe. The last player holding cards is the Bhabhi.

    To play: swipe a card up onto the table, or tap it twice.
